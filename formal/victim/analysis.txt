Why the victim cache may never lose to NINE — the exclusivity coverage argument
================================================================================

Observation across every param config swept so far: the search is UNSAT — no
trace makes the victim design incur more DRAM lookups than NINE. This is not a
coincidence of small sizes; there is a structural reason, and it suggests the
hypothesis H (C_victim <= C_NINE) may be a theorem, not merely a bounded result.

The mechanism
-------------
Both designs share the exact same L2 (same ways, same LRU trajectory, same
per-step l2_hit). So the cost gap is decided entirely on L2-miss steps, by how
often the mid level (NINE's L3 vs. the victim cache) holds the accessed line:

    gap = C_victim - C_NINE
        = #(NINE-L3 hits on L2-miss steps) - #(victim hits on L2-miss steps)

The key asymmetry is what set of DISTINCT lines each mid level can cover:

  - Victim cache is EXCLUSIVE: no line is ever in both L2 and the victim cache.
    So (L2 contents) and (victim contents) are disjoint, and together they cover
    up to w2 + w3 distinct lines.

  - NINE's L3 is INDEPENDENT (non-inclusive, non-exclusive): a line can sit in
    both L2 and L3 at once. Those duplicated lines consume L3 capacity without
    extending coverage. So L2-union-L3 covers AT MOST w2 + w3 distinct lines, and
    strictly fewer whenever any line is duplicated across L2 and L3.

On an L2-miss step the accessed line is, by definition, not in L2. Whether the
mid level saves the DRAM lookup depends on whether that line is in the mid level.
Because the victim design devotes its entire w3 to lines NOT in L2, it has more
distinct non-L2 lines cached than NINE does (whose L3 wastes slots on L2
duplicates). More distinct non-L2 coverage => the victim hits at least as often
on exactly the steps that matter => gap <= 0.

Why this doesn't depend on size
--------------------------------
The argument is about coverage of distinct lines, not about trace length or
alphabet size. Growing N, K, w2, or w3 does not create a slot where NINE covers a
non-L2 line the victim does not; exclusivity always gives the victim the larger
(or equal) distinct non-L2 footprint. That is why every config returns UNSAT.

The caveat (why this is an argument, not yet a proof)
-----------------------------------------------------
Coverage of distinct lines is necessary but the timing of hits also depends on
LRU recency order, and the victim swap reorders recency differently than NINE's
promote-on-hit. A full proof must show recency effects can never flip an
individual step in NINE's favor often enough to overcome the coverage advantage.
The bounded UNSAT results are evidence this never happens; a hand proof would
formalize the coverage-plus-recency invariant. Until then, report UNSAT with the
explicit (N, K, w2, w3) bound.

Result: the recency worry does not materialize (bounded check)
--------------------------------------------------------------
The caveat above is now partly resolved. We formalized the coverage claim as a
STEPWISE invariant, stronger than the aggregate counting argument:

    at every step,  (L3 \ L2) subset victim

i.e. every REAL line (label >= 0, excluding empty sentinels) that NINE's L3
covers but L2 does not is also resident in the victim cache. If this holds, then
on any L2-miss step the accessed line is not in L2, so l3_hit => victim_hit:
victim saves every DRAM lookup NINE saves, term by term, hence gap <= 0. This is
strictly stronger than "victim has the larger distinct non-L2 footprint" because
it pins the coverage at each individual timestep, closing exactly the recency-
reordering hole (victim's swap vs. NINE's promote-on-hit) named above.

We first checked whether this invariant can be VIOLATED on any state reachable
in the unrolled model. At Params(w2=2, w3=3, N=10, K=6) the result is:

    can the coverage invariant be VIOLATED on a reachable state?  UNSAT

So the invariant holds at every reachable step: the recency reordering never
evicts from the victim cache a non-L2 line that NINE's L3 still holds. Stepwise
dominance holds within this bound.

Result: the invariant is TRUE but NOT INDUCTIVE
----------------------------------------------
A size-independent theorem needs the INDUCTIVE step, with no horizon: for
ARBITRARY states satisfying the invariant plus physical well-formedness (all
slots pairwise distinct; real labels a MRU-side prefix, empty sentinels the
suffix; L2/victim exclusivity), one shared transition for a free access must
preserve the invariant. Base case (cold-start init, all caches empty) is trivial.
The inductive step
   invariant(states_t) AND transitions  =>  invariant(states_{t+1})
comes back SAT at every (w2,w3) tried [(2,3),(2,4),(3,4),(4,6)]: the invariant is
NOT inductive.

Every counterexample is an UNREACHABLE pre-state. Worked example at w2=2,w3=3:

    pre:  L2=[5,4]  L3=[0,1,2]  V=[2,0,1]   access=3   (invariant holds:
          L3\L2={0,1,2} subset V={0,1,2}, exclusive, well-formed)
    step: access 3 is an L2 miss; L2 evictee = LRU = 4.
          NINE  inserts 3, evicts its LRU 2   -> L3n=[3,0,1]
          victim admits evictee 4, evicts its LRU 1 -> Vn=[4,2,0]
    post: L3n\L2n = {0,1};  1 is NOT in Vn={4,2,0}  -> invariant BROKEN.

We verified this pre-state is unreachable in the real unrolled model (asking the
model to reach L2=[5,4],L3=[0,1,2],V=[2,0,1] at any t is UNSAT). So the invariant
is TRUE on reachable states but not SELF-PROPAGATING from arbitrary states.

Diagnosis: the two mid levels order recency by different keys — NINE by access
recency (promote-on-hit), victim by eviction recency (the L2 evictee enters at
MRU) — so their LRU victims diverge. The set-level predicate (L3\L2) subset
victim captures COVERAGE but not the RECENCY RELATIONSHIP between the two
structures, and coverage alone is not closed under one step. This is exactly the
recency-coupling hole flagged in "The caveat" above, now made concrete.

Where this leaves the claim
---------------------------
- BOUNDED: H (C_victim <= C_NINE) holds for all traces at each swept
  (N,K,w2,w3); the coverage invariant holds on all reachable states in the bound.
  This is solid and hand-checkable.
- THEOREM: still open. The set-invariant is not the inductive certificate. A
  proof needs a STRENGTHENED RELATIONAL invariant that also constrains recency
  positions (tying when a line is safe-from-eviction in NINE's L3 to its
  protection in victim), iterated until the step is UNSAT.
  Finding that invariant is the crux, not a mechanical tweak.

Until the strengthened step passes, report UNSAT with the explicit
(N, K, w2, w3) bound.
